# Team Lead and edges

A Team is a set of Agents working on the same thing. One of them is the **Team lead**
(人). You choose the lead by hand when you start the Team; Ronin never guesses it from
what an Agent does.

## Talk to the lead

Give the work to the lead and let the lead give it out. The lead knows who is on the
Team, hands each Agent its part, collects what comes back, and tells you where things
stand. You can still type to any Agent in its tile, but the lead is the one place to ask
"how is it going?"

## How Agents talk to each other

Agents do not need you to carry messages between them. Ronin gives each one tools for
crossing its **edges**, the boundary between one session and the next:

- **Tell** sends one message to one Agent. It arrives in that Agent's tile as if typed, with who sent it.
- **The wipeboard** is the Team's shared board, for things everyone must see: a rule, two Agents about to change the same file. Posts expire once read; nothing lasting lives there.
- **Read** looks at another Agent's record, or its live screen when it has no record yet.

You can read the wipeboard and the Messages tab in the Team's Commons to see what passed
between them.
