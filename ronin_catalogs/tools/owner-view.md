## Usage

**Ronin Services feature.** `owner_view <tab>` → what the owner is looking at in that browser tab: workbench, team, selected workspace, what each visible workspace shows, the Help page being read. Absent or stale view = 404: ask the owner to press a Help stone again in that tab.
