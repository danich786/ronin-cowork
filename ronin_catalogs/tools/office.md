## Usage

`office read` prints this office as it is now, assembled from Ronin's own records each time:
the people as the Business Lounge's About You has them (an empty About You says it is not filled
in yet) and the notes on them (`~/ronin/library/ABOUT_USER.md`); the model providers signed in;
each Workspace with its Summary (its doc, `~/ronin/library/workspaces/<handle>.md`) and the Teams
and Agents running in it; Agents running outside a Team; the services connected; the defaults
new Agents get; and, in a hosted office, the account from the Business Lounge. Read it before you
talk about the office, its people or their projects. Read-only. `office --help`.
